Our Story

Every love story deserves its own opening scene

Riya Invites began with a simple belief: a wedding invitation shouldn't just announce a date — it should feel like the first frame of a film only the two of you could star in.

We work closely with Tamil couples across Malaysia to bring their story to life — culture, language, and all — in a format made for the way we celebrate today: shared, watched, and re-watched.
